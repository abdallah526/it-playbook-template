# IT Organization and Roles

## Head of IT

* Owns the IT strategy and budget.
* Approves major decisions.

## IT Manager

* Runs daily operations and escalations.

## Team Lead

* Plans and reviews the team's work.
* Owns this playbook.

## Engineers (System / Network)

* Run servers, network and security.

## Helpdesk

* First line of support for employees.

{% hint style="info" %}
Rename these roles to match your team, and keep the titles identical to the Team Directory.
{% endhint %}
