# Office Activation

### Prerequisites

* A PC with Microsoft Office installed (e.g., Office 2016, 2019, or 2021).
* A valid product key (25-character code) or a Microsoft account with an active subscription.
* Administrative privileges on the PC.
* Internet connection (for traditional method).

### Steps

#### Activate with a product key

1. **Open an Office Application**:
   * Launch an Office application (e.g., Word or Excel).
2. **Start Activation**:
   * If prompted, sign in with a Microsoft account linked to your Office license or enter the product key when requested.
   * Go to "File" > "Account" > "Activate Product" and follow the prompts to enter the key (e.g., XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX).
3. **Complete Activation**:
   * If online, Office will connect to Microsoft’s activation servers.
   * If offline, select "Activate by phone" and follow the prompts to contact Microsoft support.
4. **Verify Activation**:
   * Return to "File" > "Account" and confirm "Product Activated" is displayed.

{% hint style="info" %}
Ensure the product key matches your Office edition (e.g., Professional Plus). If activation fails, contact your IT admin to verify the product key.
{% endhint %}
