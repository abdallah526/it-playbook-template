# Windows Activation

### Prerequisites

* A Windows PC (e.g., Windows 10 or 11) with an installed operating system.
* A valid product key (25-character code) or a digital license tied to your Microsoft account or organization.
* Administrative privileges on the PC.
* Internet connection (for traditional method).

### Steps

#### Activate with a product key

1. **Open Activation Settings**:
   * Press `Win + I` to open Settings.
   * Navigate to "Update & Security" > "Activation."
2. **Check Activation Status**:
   * Verify if Windows is currently activated. If not, you’ll see a message like "Windows is not activated."
3. **Enter Product Key**:
   * Click "Change product key" or "Activate."
   * Enter the 25-character product key (e.g., XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX) provided by your organization or purchased from Microsoft.
   * Click "Next" to proceed.
4. **Activate Windows**:
   * If online, Windows will automatically connect to Microsoft’s activation servers.
   * If offline, select "Activate by phone" and follow the prompts to contact Microsoft support.
5. **Verify Activation**:
   * Return to the Activation settings and confirm "Windows is activated" with your product ID displayed.

{% hint style="info" %}
Ensure the product key matches the Windows edition (e.g., Pro, Enterprise). If activation fails, contact your IT admin to verify the product key.
{% endhint %}
