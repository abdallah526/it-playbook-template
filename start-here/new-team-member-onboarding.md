# New Team Member Onboarding

Welcome to the team. This page tells you what to read, in what order, and which accounts to ask for. Work through it during your training, then keep the playbook as your reference.

## Day 1: The team and the rules

* [IT Organization and Roles](../it-department/it-organization-and-roles.md) and the [Team Directory](../it-department/team-directory.md)
* [Approval Workflow](../policies-and-approvals/approval-workflow.md) and [Task Approval Matrix](../policies-and-approvals/task-approval-matrix.md)

## Day 1: Accounts to ask for

* Password manager
* Directory account (for example Active Directory) and company email
* Ticketing system
* Remote support tool
* Team chat and notification channels

## Week 1: How the company runs

* [Network Overview](../network-and-infrastructure/network-overview.md) and the [site pages](../network-and-infrastructure/sites/README.md)
* The Quick Start of every system in **Systems & Tools**

## Week 2: Keeping things running

* **Monitoring & Alerts**: what each alert means
* **Incidents & Troubleshooting**: read every runbook before you need it
* **How-To Guides**: start with user onboarding and offboarding

## End of training checklist

* [ ] I can sign in to every system above
* [ ] I know who to escalate to for each area
* [ ] I have handled a ticket from start to finish
* [ ] I know what each monitoring alert means
* [ ] I have walked through one runbook with a team member

## When you are stuck later

1. Search the playbook.
2. Check **Incidents & Troubleshooting** and the Troubleshooting page of the system.
3. Still stuck? Ask the team, then add the answer to the playbook.
