# Device & Software Requests

## Scope

Which items this covers (laptops, monitors, phones, licenses, software) and who can request them.

## How to request

1. The employee or their manager opens a ticket with the item and the business reason.
2. IT checks stock or licenses and adds the cost if a purchase is needed.

## Approval

Who approves each type of request. Keep this in line with the [Task Approval Matrix](task-approval-matrix.md).

## Delivery and return

* Record the device or license against the employee when you hand it over.
* Collect devices and free licenses when the employee leaves (see the offboarding checklists).
