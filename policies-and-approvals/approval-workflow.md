# Approval Workflow

## Roles

* **Requester**: opens the ticket.
* **Approver**: approves or rejects.
* **Executor**: does the work and closes the ticket.

## Flow

1. The requester opens a ticket.
2. The approver approves or rejects.
3. The executor does the work and updates the ticket.
