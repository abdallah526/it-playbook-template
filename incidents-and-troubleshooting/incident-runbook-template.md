# Incident Runbook Template

## When to use this runbook

## Impact

Who is affected and how badly.

## Steps

1. Tell the team and the affected users.
2. Check the obvious causes first.
3. Apply the fix or the workaround.
4. Confirm everything is back.

## Escalation

| After | Escalate to |
| --- | --- |
| 15 minutes | Team Lead |
| 1 hour | IT Manager |

## After the incident

Write what happened and what to change.
