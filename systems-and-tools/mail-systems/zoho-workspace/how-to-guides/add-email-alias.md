# Add Email Alias

### Prerequisites

* Access to the Zoho Mail admin panel with administrative privileges.
* The user’s email account must be active in the Zoho Mail organization.
* A valid domain registered in Zoho Mail.

### Steps

1. Log in to the Zoho Mail admin panel.
2. Click on "Users" under the "Domains" section.
3.  Select the user from the list and click the three-dot menu, then choose "Add email alias".<br>

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (62).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
4. Navigate to "Mailbox Settings" and click on "Email Alias".
5. Click the "+ Add" button.
6. Enter the display name in the "Display Name" field.
7. Enter the email alias in the "Email alias" field.
8. Check the "Set as Mailbox Address" box if needed.
9. Click the "Add" button.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (63).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
Ensure the alias domain is registered and verified in Zoho Mail. Coordinate with your IT admin to align with organizational email policies. If the alias fails to save or work, verify the domain settings and check with your IT admin for assistance.
{% endhint %}
