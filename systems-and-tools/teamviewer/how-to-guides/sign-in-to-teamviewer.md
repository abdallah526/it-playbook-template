# Sign In to TeamViewer

### Prerequisites

* TeamViewer desktop application installed on your device.
* TeamViewer account credentials:
  * Email: it-team@example.com
  * Password: Stored in Keeper Security (retrieve from Keeper using your credentials).
* A web browser to access the verification email if needed.

### Steps

1. **Open TeamViewer Desktop Application**:
   * Launch the TeamViewer application on your device.
2. **Navigate to Computers & Contacts**:
   * In the TeamViewer main window, click **Computers & Contacts** from the left sidebar to access the management section.
3.  **Sign In to TeamViewer Account**:

    * Click **Sign In** in the Computers & Contacts section.
    * Enter the TeamViewer account email: it-team@example.com.
    * Retrieve the password from Keeper Security and enter it in the **Password** field.
    * Check the box for **Keep me signed in** to avoid repeated logins.
    * Click **Sign In** to log in to the account.

    <figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (266).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
4. **Verify Device Assignment (First-Time Sign-In)**:
   * If this is the first time signing in from this device, a confirmation pop-up may appear, stating that a verification email has been sent to it-team@example.com.
   * Check the inbox (or junk/spam folder) for the email and follow the link to confirm the device assignment.
5.  **Manage and Access Employee Devices**:

    * After signing in, the **Computers & Contacts** section will display a list of managed devices (e.g., PC159, PC130).
    * Expand sections like **Recent Connections**, **Device Groups**, or **My Managed Devices** to view employee devices.
    * Double-click a device (e.g., PC116) to initiate a remote access session.

    <figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (267).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
Ensure the device is online during the sign-in process. If the verification email is not received, check the spam/junk folder or verify the email address with the IT admin. Keep the TeamViewer password secure in Keeper Security.
{% endhint %}
